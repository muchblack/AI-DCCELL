# Channel: Threads (casual)

Read with `brand-voice.md` → 核心 + Threads sections. The 範文 there are the
source of truth for how casual "casual" is. These rules only stop the
common ways AI fakes it.

## Voice

Casual = how the author actually talks in the 範文. It is **not** a
performance of casualness. Reject:

- interjections or particles (欸、啦、der、笑死、真的會…) beyond what the 範文
  use;
- more than 1 emoji per post unless the 範文 clearly use more;
- hook formulas: 「你知道嗎？」「99% 的人不知道」「這件事改變了我」;
- bait endings: 「你們覺得呢？」「留言告訴我」, unless it's a real question the
  author wants answered;
- listicle structure (1. 2. 3. with bold heads); a post is a thought, not a
  slide;
- a tidy moral at the end. It's fine to stop on an open or unresolved thought.

## Format

- Hard limit 500 characters per post. Aim for ≤300. Longer → split into a
  reply chain and mark each part `（1/n）` in the draft.
- At most 1 topic tag.
- A link only when the post is about that link; put it last.

## Images

Source priority:

| image | Use when | Who |
|-------|----------|-----|
| `own` | Default. Experience, daily life, work notes: author's photo or screenshot | user supplies |
| `card` | One line or a short list worth emphasising | main renders: white background, black text, one system font, no decoration |
| `generated` | Only for concepts or metaphors | main via `/hf-image`; draft says it's generated |

**Hard rule: never generate the author's own life.** No AI images of the
author's desk, places they went, food they ate, their screen or their
people. That fabricates experience the same way invented anecdotes do.
Such a request → `own` with an `image_note` saying what to shoot.

Screenshots: the `image_note` always reminds the user to blur names, faces
and internal company info.

Image-first posts (quick Step 2a): the user's idea is the meaning; the
image description only supplies concrete detail.
