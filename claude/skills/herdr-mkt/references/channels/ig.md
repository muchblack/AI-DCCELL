## Decided

- **Caption = exhibit label.** Structure:
  ```
  <標題>
  <日期>・<媒材／場景>

  <2–3 行短文>
  ```
  Restrained wording, no exclamation marks, no emoji.
- **Visual consistency beats single-image polish.** The grid must read as one
  exhibition: fixed margins, type and palette from one HTML template, with
  only the content changing. `/hf-image` output may be material inside the
  template, never the finished post.
- Hashtags: ≤5, placed after the caption.
- Image-first posts work the same as Threads (`modes.md` quick Step 2a),
  including the privacy and GPS checks.
